## About Me

I work with data end to end — from messy spreadsheets and raw tables to dashboards and insights a non-technical person can actually use.

My focus is not just making charts, but finding the answer behind the numbers: what changed, why it changed, and what should happen next.

- 🧹 Cleaning and preparing data before any analysis
- 📊 Building dashboards and reports that track what matters
- 🔍 Exploring data to find patterns, gaps, and opportunities
- 💡 Explaining results simply, so decisions become easy

## How I Work

1. **Clean first, analyse second** — good insights need reliable data.
2. **Show, don't just tell** — one clear chart beats a long paragraph.
3. **Keep it simple** — if a stakeholder can't understand it in a minute, it's not finished yet.
